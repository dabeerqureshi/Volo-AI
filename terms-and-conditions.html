1. The service

Volo AI provides an AI voice receptionist service for UK businesses. Volo answers incoming calls on your existing number after a 5-second ringing window, books appointments, handles enquiries and FAQs, saves data to your connected database and delivers call transcripts to you.

2. Plans & billing

  • 14-day free trial: full access for 14 days, no card required.
  • Lite: £29/month — 1 phone number, 50 calls included, £0.15/min overage.
  • Business: £79/month — 1 phone number, 300 calls included, £0.12/min overage.
  • Pro: £129/month — up to 3 phone numbers, unlimited calls, £0.10/min overage.
  • Annual: pay for 10 months, get 12 — £290 / £790 / £1,290 per year.

All prices are in GBP (£), billed monthly or annually in advance. Calls beyond the included allowance are billed at the overage rate for your plan. The free trial converts to the selected paid plan automatically unless cancelled before the trial ends.
