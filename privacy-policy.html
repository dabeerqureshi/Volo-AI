Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who we are

Volo AI provides an AI voice receptionist service for UK businesses. We answer calls, book appointments, handle enquiries and FAQs, save data to your chosen database and deliver transcripts.

Contact: hello@voloai.uk · WhatsApp: +92 314 4781120

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, business name, email, phone, billing details.
  • Call data: audio recordings, transcripts, appointment and enquiry details.
  • Integration data: access to connected databases (Airtable, Google Sheets, etc.).
  • Website data: server logs, essential cookies, anonymised analytics.

3. How we use data

  • To provide the Volo AI service.
  • To write appointments into your connected tools.
  • To send service notifications and billing receipts.
  • To improve quality. Audio is never used to train models for other customers.

4. Legal basis

We process call data to deliver the service you ask us to provide (contract performance) and to protect legitimate business interests, such as keeping accurate records and dealing with enquiries. Call recording and AI processing rely on legitimate interests; where recording is used for marketing or training, we ask for consent. Where we process sensitive (special category) data, we rely on an additional appropriate basis and protect it accordingly.

A Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) is maintained for our automated voice processing, as required by UK GDPR. Data can be hosted entirely in the UK on request.

5. Sharing & sub-processors

We never sell data. We share it only with essential sub-processors who help deliver the service — telephony (e.g. Twilio), voice AI & speech processing (e.g. Vapi), workflow automation (e.g. n8n), email/SMS delivery and hosting. Each has a Data Processing Agreement, processes data only on our instructions, and may not use it for AI training or any other purpose.

6. Call recording notice

The AI greeting informs callers at the start of every call that the call may be recorded. Under UK law (Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000) recording for legitimate business purposes is lawful, and we balance it against callers' interests. Recordings and transcripts belong to the business that owns the number and are used to provide the service and keep records — never to train models for other customers. If a caller is uncomfortable being recorded, they can ask to speak to a person or have the call ended.

7. Security

Voice streams and transcripts are encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256). Access is restricted to staff who need it for support, and access attempts are logged. We review our security controls regularly.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may access, rectify, erase, restrict, port or object to processing of your data. We respond to subject access requests within 30 days. Email hello@voloai.uk.

9. Retention & deletion

Call audio/transcripts: 12 months by default, configurable on request. Account data: deleted or returned within 30 days after cancellation. Recordings and transcripts are deleted on request at any time.

10. Cookies

Essential cookies only. No third-party advertising cookies.

11. Changes

Material changes notified 14 days in advance by email or in-dashboard notice.
