// everything you need

More than an answering machine.

Volo doesn't just answer — it books, captures leads, and tells you what you're missing.

Always-on receptionist

Answers your number 24/7 in clear, natural English. Even on Sundays, bank holidays, and after hours.

Appointment booking

Books appointments straight into Calendly, Google Sheets, or Airtable — with the customer's name, number, and preferred time.

Instant FAQ & knowledge base

Pre-loaded with your prices, policies and opening hours — or upload your own documents and Volo learns them. No more "I'll call you back to check".

Opening hours aware

Volo knows when you're open and closed. Outside hours it takes a message, captures a lead, or books a slot for the next available day.

Caller info capture

Automatically captures name, number, email and the reason for calling — then confirms by SMS or email. Every lead lands in your spreadsheet or CRM.

Smart routing & handoff

Rules decide who goes where — sales, support or emergencies. On Pro, Volo hands complex calls to your team with a full summary, so nobody repeats themselves.

Call transcripts & summaries

Every call transcribed and emailed to you. Includes a one-line summary: what the caller wanted and what happened.

Weekly "what you missed" report

Every Monday: how many calls Volo handled, how many appointments were booked, and how many calls would have been missed without it.

Your voice, your tone

Pick a voice, accent and personality, or record your own greeting. Formal for a law firm, friendly for a salon — Volo sounds like your business.

Analytics dashboard

See call volume, no-answer rates and bookings at a glance — and search any past conversation by date or keyword.

Plug into your stack

Calendly, Google Sheets, Airtable, HubSpot, Outlook, Slack and 200+ more — powered by the same workflow engine that runs large AI contact centres.

Multilingual

Natural UK English out of the box, plus additional languages on request for multilingual teams and customers.
